# Gloria Union English Fan Translation v0.25

Version 0.25 is the first story-playable English release candidate.

## Highlights

- English main-story dialogue
- English opening narration
- Localized cards, requirements, items, equipment, and character notices
- Localized pre-battle, battle, Study, victory, and stage-clear interfaces
- Localized save/load, status, map, unit, card, and character-selection screens
- English Chapter 1 and BF1-1 title artwork
- Translation credit added to the opening movie

## Known limitations

- Some chapter and scenario splash-screen title artwork remains in Korean.
- A small amount of Korean may remain in short battle taunts, decorative artwork,
  uncommon menu paths, or later unlockable content.
- Character Select uses the compact label `MR` for morale because the original
  interface has a fixed-width text slot.
- This release does not claim that every rasterized Korean word is replaced. The
  main story and essential gameplay information are readable in English.

## Distribution

The public download will be a patch that requires the user to provide an original
copy of the game. The complete modified APK is intentionally excluded from this
repository and its public releases.

# Known limitations

The v0.25 translation is deliberately described as story-playable rather than
100% text-complete.

- New chapters and scenarios can display Korean title artwork during their intro.
- Some short battle taunts and character-name presentations may remain Korean.
- Decorative or rarely visited interface artwork can still contain Korean text.
- Later unlockable characters and items have received less in-game visual testing
  than the main story path.

These items do not prevent the main plot from being followed through the English
dialogue boxes. Future releases can replace the remaining rasterized text in
smaller, targeted batches.

# Gloria Union English Fan Translation v0.25

This is the first story-playable public release of the English fan translation
for the Android version of *Gloria Union*.

The main story can be followed through the English dialogue boxes. Core menus,
battle instructions, card descriptions and requirements, item descriptions,
character information, equipment screens, and most result screens have also
been localized.

## Download and patching

Download `GloriaUnion-English-v0.25-Patch-Windows.zip` and extract it. Double-click
`PATCH_WINDOWS.bat`, then drag the supported original APK into the window.

The download contains no complete game APK. The patcher requires the user's own
original APK and checks its full SHA-256 before producing the translated version.

Supported original APK SHA-256:

`e10783d344c7a2fb8c229d224d20953946f1c91caac7eaa87fa7ce1fa9829c5e`

## Highlights

- English main-story dialogue and opening narration
- Localized cards, requirements, items, equipment, and character notices
- Localized pre-battle, battle, Study, victory, and stage-clear interfaces
- Localized save/load, status, map, unit, card, and character-selection screens
- English Chapter 1 and BF1-1 title artwork
- Translation credit in the opening movie

## Known limitations

- Some chapter and scenario splash-screen title artwork remains in Korean.
- Scattered Korean may remain in short battle taunts, decorative artwork,
  uncommon menu paths, or later unlockable content.
- Character Select abbreviates morale as `MR` because of its fixed-width slot.
- The release is story-playable, not every-raster-complete.
